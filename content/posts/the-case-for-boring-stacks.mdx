The team that ships is the team that chose the dullest tool in the room. I've watched three rewrites stall on shiny infrastructure picks that turned into year-long migrations.

## The test I apply now

Before adopting a new piece of infrastructure, I ask three questions:

1. What does this replace, and does the replacement actually hurt today?
2. Who owns it when the person who chose it leaves?
3. Would a boring version work for twelve more months?

If the answers aren't convincing, the answer is Postgres.
