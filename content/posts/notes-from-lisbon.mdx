Lisbon isn't a city you optimise. Everything's uphill, the trams are slower than walking, and the best pastéis de nata are always five streets away from wherever you happen to be standing.

## What surprised me

- 8pm is early for dinner.
- The Time Out Market is fine but the neighbourhood places around it are better and half the price.
- Cascais by train is the best half-day off.
