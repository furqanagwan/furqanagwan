The highest-leverage financial decisions I've made had nothing to do with stock picks. They were procedural: automating savings, defaulting pension contributions up, and refusing to check balances more than once a month.

## The three-account setup

One current account for bills, one for day-to-day spending, one for investing. Direct debits route between them on payday. You never make the choice twice.

## What I stopped doing

- Checking portfolio value during work hours.
- Reading finance Twitter.
- Optimising cashback on cards instead of optimising income.
