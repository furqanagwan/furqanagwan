## The interview is a two-way test

Treat every round as you vetting the team. Ask about on-call, code review, and how decisions actually get made. The answers you hear at interview time are usually the best the team is willing to perform — whatever's below that is what you'll live with.

## What actually matters in the first 90 days

- Ship something small to prod in week one. Unblocking the toolchain is the real onboarding.
- Write down every assumption you make. You'll be wrong about half of them, and that list is how you learn the system.
- Find the person who's been there longest and the person who joined most recently. Together they tell you what's stable and what's shifting.
